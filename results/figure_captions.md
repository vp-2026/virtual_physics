# Figure captions

**Figure 3. Performance during interaction and the benefit of feedback.** A: Initial and final performance under full visual feedback, on 1641 common tasks for action and chosen-action prediction and 116 matched tasks for novel prediction. Novel pre/post predictions use the same untried placement. B: Full-feedback minus retry-only performance after the same failed first action; novel predictions are compared after exactly one rollout. Points are means; intervals are 95% layout-bootstrap confidence intervals. Initial-to-final changes alone do not identify a causal feedback benefit.

**Figure 4. Correspondence depends on the ability pair and observation checkpoint.** Top: Observed prediction quality by action outcome (A, C), and terminal chosen/novel prediction pairs with descriptive linear fits (B). Bottom: A, chosen-PQ difference controlling exact goal and attempt, with equal episode weights; B, partial rank correlations controlling layout/direction (filled) and additionally baseline predictions and rollout count (open); C, baseline-adjusted novel-PQ difference after exactly one rollout, controlling layout/direction. A uses 1641 tasks; B-C use 116 matched tasks. Intervals are 95% layout-bootstrap confidence intervals. These are conditional associations, not causal effects of success.
